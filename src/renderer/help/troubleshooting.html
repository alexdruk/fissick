Troubleshooting

Solutions to common problems

Photos still show wrong dates after import

This is the most common question. There are two things to check:

Check the status tag in Fossick

Open the Photos & Videos tab and find the affected photos. If they show Filename date or No date, the sidecar wasn't found for those photos and the original date couldn't be written. This can happen when the sidecar file has an unexpected name format. See "No sidecar found" below.

Make sure you're importing the right files

Always import your photos from a Fossick export folder, not from the original Takeout ZIP. The Takeout files still have the wrong metadata — Fossick's copies are the fixed ones. Use the Copy Fixed Files or Export by Date export option first, then import those files to Apple Photos or Lightroom.

Apple Photos specific

Apple Photos sometimes ignores the DateTimeOriginal EXIF field and uses the file creation date instead. To prevent this, use Export by Date — the folder timestamps also help Apple Photos read the date correctly. If photos still appear on the wrong date, try dragging just a few into Photos first to confirm before importing the full library.

A lot of photos show "No sidecar found"

If more than a few percent of your photos show Filename date or No date, the most common causes are:

  • You only downloaded some of the Takeout ZIPs. Google splits large exports across multiple ZIP files. Each ZIP might contain photos from some years and sidecars from others. Make sure you downloaded all the ZIPs from your export. Check your email — Google sends a separate download link for each ZIP.
  • The sidecar was in a different part of the export. Album folders often contain copies of photos, but the sidecar might only be in the year folder. Fossick handles this automatically when all ZIPs are loaded together.
  • The photos were uploaded without metadata. Photos uploaded to Google Photos via a third-party app, or synced from certain cameras, sometimes arrive without sidecar files. In this case, Fossick's filename-date fallback is the best available option.

Processing is taking a very long time

Writing EXIF metadata to thousands of photo files is genuinely slow — it's a hardware operation, not a software one. Fossick processes 20 photos at a time, which is the maximum that allows good throughput without overwhelming your system.

  • Let it run overnight. For large libraries (50,000+ photos), multi-hour processing is normal. Fossick continues in the background.
  • Make sure your drive isn't nearly full. If your startup disk is more than 90% full, write performance degrades significantly. Free up space or point Fossick at an external drive with more space.
  • Plug in your charger. On battery power, macOS and Windows reduce CPU performance. Processing is faster when plugged in.

Fossick won't open (macOS)

On macOS, you may see a warning that Fossick "cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer." This is a macOS security feature, not a sign that Fossick is malicious.

To open it: right-click (or Control-click) the Fossick app icon and choose Open from the menu. You'll see the same warning, but this time with an Open button. Click it. macOS will remember your choice and Fossick will open normally from then on.

Fossick won't open (Windows)

On Windows, you may see a SmartScreen warning: "Windows protected your PC." Click More info, then Run anyway. This warning appears for apps that haven't yet accumulated enough download history with Microsoft — it doesn't mean the app is unsafe.

Location data or Trips aren't showing

Trips and the Places map require GPS coordinates in your photos or a Location History export from Google.

  • Check your photo status tags. If most photos show no GPS tag, your photos don't have location data — likely because the camera or phone had location turned off.
  • Did you export Location History? If you want the full location track (including times when you weren't taking photos), you need to re-export from Google Takeout and include Location History. See Getting Started.
  • Try Recompute. If you've added new photos, click the Recompute button in the Trips or Places tab to regenerate the groupings.

I need more help

If you're stuck on something not covered here, email support@fossick.app with a description of the problem and, if possible, a screenshot of the error. Response time is typically within one business day.

Photos & Videos

Browsing your photo library, understanding status tags, and selecting photos

The Photos & Videos tab

After processing, this is your main view. Every photo and video from your Google Takeout is shown here in a date-sorted grid, from newest to oldest. You can filter, search, select, and export from this view.

Status tags

Every photo has a coloured tag that tells you what happened to its metadata during processing.

EXIF fixed Filename date No date GPS
TagWhat it means
EXIF fixedFossick found the sidecar file for this photo and wrote the original date (and GPS, if present) into the photo. This is the ideal outcome. The photo will import to any app with the correct date.
Filename dateNo sidecar was found, but Fossick extracted the date from the filename itself — for example, a file named PXL_20220815_143022.jpg contains the date in the name. The date written is correct but GPS coordinates are not available.
No dateNo sidecar was found and the filename didn't contain a recognisable date. The photo will appear in your archive but without a correct date. See Troubleshooting for help with these.
GPSThis photo has GPS coordinates — either from the sidecar or already embedded in the file. It will appear on maps in the Trips and Places views.

Filtering your library

Use the filter tabs at the top of the grid to narrow your view:

  • All — shows every photo and video
  • Fixed — only photos with EXIF fixed status
  • No sidecar — photos where no sidecar was found (Filename date + No date)
  • No date — photos with no date at all
  • With GPS — photos that have location data

You can also filter by date range using the date pickers at the top right, and by file type (photos only / videos only / both).

Selecting photos

Click any photo to select it. A selected photo shows an orange border and a checkmark. You can select multiple photos by clicking each one, or hold ⌘ (Mac) / Ctrl (Windows) while clicking. To select all visible photos, press ⌘A / Ctrl+A.

Selections are used for targeted exports — for example, you might select only your Paris photos and export just those to a folder. See Exporting.

Lightbox view

Click the expand icon on a photo thumbnail (or double-click the photo) to open it in the lightbox — a full-screen view showing the photo at full resolution alongside its metadata: date, filename, GPS coordinates (if available), album membership, and status.

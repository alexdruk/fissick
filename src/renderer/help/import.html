Import

How to bring your Google Takeout data into Fossick

Two ways to import

Fossick can work with your Takeout data in two ways: as the original ZIP files Google sends you, or as an already-extracted folder. Both work equally well — use whichever is more convenient.

Option A: Drop the ZIP files directly

This is the recommended approach. You don't need to unzip anything. Drag all your Takeout ZIP files (they're usually named something like takeout-20240312-001.zip, takeout-20240312-002.zip, and so on) straight onto the Fossick import area. You can drop them all at once.

Option B: Point to an extracted folder

If you've already unzipped your Takeout files, click Choose Folder and select the top-level Takeout folder. Fossick will scan it automatically.

Multi-part exports

Google splits large libraries across multiple ZIP files. These all belong together — each ZIP might contain photos from different years or albums. Fossick automatically merges them. Just add all the ZIPs at once; don't process them one at a time.

What Fossick looks for inside your export

Google Takeout organises your photos into folders like Photos from 2022, Photos from 2023, and so on, plus folders for each album. Alongside every photo, there's a small companion file — the sidecar — that contains the original date and GPS coordinates.

Fossick scans all of these automatically. You don't need to reorganise anything or rename any files before importing.

Including your location history

If you also exported your Location History from Google (see Getting Started), Fossick will find it automatically inside the Takeout folder. Location history powers the Trips and Places features. If you didn't include it in your export, those sections will still work — they'll just use the GPS coordinates stored in your photos instead.

Missing some years?

If you notice photos from certain years are missing, you may need to request a new export. Google Takeout sometimes doesn't include all photos in a single export for very large libraries. You can import a second export into Fossick and it will merge the results.

After adding your files

Once you've added your ZIPs or folder, you'll see a summary of what Fossick found: how many photos, whether location history was detected, and whether albums are present. Click Start Processing when you're ready. See Processing for what happens next.
